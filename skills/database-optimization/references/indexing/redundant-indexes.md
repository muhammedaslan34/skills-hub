# Redundant and unused indexes
Compare key order, expressions, predicates, collation, operator classes, uniqueness, included columns, sort direction and constraint dependencies. Identical prefixes are candidates, not proof of redundancy. Inspect usage across primary/replicas, representative business cycles and stats reset dates. Zero scans can hide rare critical paths. Protect primary keys, unique/exclusion constraints, foreign key support and operational workloads. Estimate write/storage benefit and check plans before proposing removal. Require approval, reversible DDL and post-change monitoring.
